# RQ2 literature notes: LLM/agent, state, graph and recommendation

Checked against the original publisher or arXiv record on 2026-09-25. This is a scoped reading note, not a systematic review. “Not established” means the cited abstract does not demonstrate the exact controlled comparison in our protocol; it does **not** mean the paper lacks any deterministic baseline or that no prior work has performed one. Detailed baseline and metric cells should be verified in each full PDF before the manuscript makes stronger claims.

| Original study | Student state and recommendation policy | Graph / candidate role | Evaluation stated in original record | Relevance and precise limit for our RQ2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Lv et al., ReAL, Findings EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.11/) | LLM uses learner profiles and item text; retrieves similar learners and reflects on learner feedback to refine item recommendation. | LLM helps generate personalized item candidates; an explicit shared prerequisite graph is not described in the abstract. | Three real-world datasets; compares recommendation accuracy, data utilization and stability with representative baselines. | Highly relevant agent-based exercise recommendation. It varies the candidate-generation and policy machinery together, so its abstract does not establish an isolated **same BKT state + same graph + same candidates** comparison of a local LLM against B+. |
| [Sheng et al., LearnerCoMPASS, ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.408/) | Dynamic cognitive-diagnosis vectors and a multi-model LLM path planner. | Dynamic knowledge graph plus Graph-RAG grounds planning. | Reports comparison with state-of-the-art baselines and ablations of diagnosis and multi-model planning. | Direct precedent for combining learner state, graph and LLMs. Its end-to-end multi-model intervention differs from holding inputs fixed while changing only the recommendation policy, and the original abstract does not establish a small local model test. |
| [Liu et al., PersonaPath, arXiv 2026; accepted AACL-IJCNLP 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18861) | Benchmark supplies learner personas/mastery and learning goals; LLM plans a path across concepts, units and textbooks. | Hierarchical prerequisite knowledge graph; 2,000 personas, 347 textbooks, 1,751 units, 4,092 concepts. | Tests representative LLMs; strongest reported Basic Education final pass rate 29.5%; adaptivity at most 44.7% in abstract. | Strong motivation to audit prerequisite adherence and adaptation independently. The task is curriculum-scale path planning with constructed personas, not our one-step recommendation on logged ASSISTments snapshots or an identified causal learning gain. Recent preprint/acceptance; cite status accurately. |
| [Li et al., CIKT, EMNLP 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.975/) | Analyst LLM builds evolving explainable learner profiles; Predictor uses them for future-response prediction; iterative optimization links them. | No prerequisite graph or candidate recommender is described in the abstract. | Evaluated predictive accuracy/explainability/scalability on multiple educational datasets. | Relevant to the distinction between state estimation and policy. It evaluates **knowledge tracing**, not the operational quality of choosing a valid next item under matched policy inputs. Our RQ2 holds the BKT state estimator fixed to isolate the policy comparison. |
| [Abu-Rasheed et al., LLM-Assisted Knowledge Graph Completion, EDUCON 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12300) | Curriculum/domain/user models; LLM assists human experts with topic extraction and graph completion. | KG built from two embedded-systems modules, with cross-course connections. | Qualitative domain-expert feedback and quantitative graph-quality metrics. | Relevant to graph provenance and expert review. It tests KG construction rather than controlled learner-level next-item policy quality; our graph must be documented as a constructed artifact, not assumed ground truth. |

## Implication for our study

Frame RQ2 as an **exploratory controlled policy comparison** in a resource-limited setting. Freeze BKT-derived latent mastery, prerequisite graph, scenario snapshots and candidate sets. Supply the identical inputs to deterministic B+ and the local LLM policy. Develop prompts, graph and candidate rules only on TRAIN/VALIDATION, then lock Gate C before RQ2 TEST. Report validity, constraint adherence, latency/errors, stability across repeated runs and a blinded rationale rubric if feasible. These operational and human-judged outcomes are **not evidence that the recommendations improve future learning** without an intervention or suitable logged-policy evaluation. Do not claim novelty as “first ever”; the defensible contribution is the narrowly controlled comparison and its transparent limits.
